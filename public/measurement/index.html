What gets counted.

This site does not measure visits or example actions. The measurement described below is switched off.

When enabled, Plausible counts page visits and reports referring sites, campaign links, approximate location, browser, and device type. It does not use analytics cookies. SeePlausible's data policyfor how it processes visits.

We also count selections in the example galleries and actions such as opening source or documentation, finding run instructions, and successfully copying commands or a share link. These events contain the example's name and section, the kind of action, and whether it happened on the homepage or an example page, as applicable.

We do not send source code, command text, or clipboard contents. Clicking a link or copying a command does not tell us whether you installed x2c or ran a program successfully.

Pages load their fonts from Google Fonts, which sends your IP address to Google.
